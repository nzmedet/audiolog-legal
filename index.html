Audiolog

Audiolog is a simple private diary app for voice-to-text note taking.

What the app does

  • Captures speech and converts it to transcript text.
  • Saves entries locally on your device.
  • Supports mood and tag organization.
  • Allows exporting your diary entries when you choose.

Privacy model

Audiolog is designed for local-first journaling. Data is stored on-device and encrypted at rest. There is no user account and no app backend for diary storage.

If the device is lost, reset, or replaced, you may lose your notes unless you exported them.
