Support

Need help with Audiolog? Send your request directly from this page.

How to Contact Support

Use the button below to open your email app with a pre-filled support draft. Include your issue in one message so we can triage faster.

What to Include

  1. Your iPhone model and iOS version
  2. Audiolog app version
  3. Expected behavior
  4. Actual behavior
  5. Steps to reproduce (if available)

Response Time

We review requests as quickly as possible. Some issues require additional investigation and follow-up.

Privacy Questions

Review the Privacy Policy first. If your question remains open, include it in your support request.
