Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 18, 2026

Overview

Audiolog is a local diary app. We do not provide cloud accounts or host diary content on our own backend.

Data we process

  • Diary transcripts created from speech recognition.
  • Entry metadata such as timestamps, mood, and tags.
  • Biometric lock preference stored on-device.

How data is stored

Diary data is stored locally on your device. Transcript content is encrypted at rest on-device.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only when you explicitly use export or iOS sharing features.

Permissions

Audiolog may request microphone and speech recognition permissions to transcribe your notes, and optional biometric permission to lock app access.

Your control

  • You can delete entries at any time in the app.
  • You can export entries using the export feature.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove local app data from the device.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: nzmedet@gmail.com
